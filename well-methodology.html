NLT143 RESEARCH

Well data and limitations

Nationwide screening from state oil and gas agencies. Not a title search, a capacity study, or an environmental opinion.

How the map is built

Gas wells come from each state's public regulator feed, drawn from vector tiles. The Wells card reads tiles/gaswells-manifest.json for the state under the map: agency, coverage, active, inactive, and plugged counts, the source refresh date, and a link to the source.

Coverage has three levels:

  • live full status: the agency file includes a status Siteline can show, plus counts.
  • partial status: the file has a status, but it does not confirm producing, operator, or dates.
  • file snapshot: a downloaded agency file, not a live query.

A state with no row in the verified catalog is labeled No public wells feed wired for this state yet and hatched. An empty hatch is not a count of zero wells.

Texas

Texas wells in this layer come from Railroad Commission of Texas GIS map symbols. The headline status is Status not confirmed (RRC map symbol). Producing status is not confirmed. The layer has no operator and no dates. Shut-in gas is labeled Inactive, shut-in (RRC). Data as of stays UNKNOWN until the source file carries a refresh date.

NETL, HIFLD, New Mexico OCD, and Colorado OGCC are not the status authority for a Texas well.

Plugged wells

Plugged counts are in the manifest and on the Wells card. The preview tiles omit plugged wells. The map draws them after the nightly build, when the plugged toggle is available. A plugged map symbol alone is not confirmed plugged and abandoned.

What the labels mean

  • Inactive is not abandoned.
  • Orphan is not abandoned.
  • Historical and missing status are not abandoned.
  • Plugged and abandoned is confirmed only with explicit official evidence from that state's regulator.
  • Kansas, Kentucky, Illinois, and Alaska non-plugged wells show as Not plugged (state reports plugged or not only).
  • Well proximity is not gas deliverability, capacity, pressure, or service.
  • Well proximity is not oil liability.
  • Where a date, operator, or status is missing, the map says UNKNOWN.

EIA natural gas pipelines are public pipeline context on their own layer. They are not wellbores.
